# 8 · Put your remotes on your dashboard

Because every remote is a real Home Assistant device, any card works. Two nice options:

## Built-in cards

*Edit dashboard → Add card*: **Thermostat** for an AC, **Tile** or **Media control** for a TV, **Tile** for a curtain.

## Roost Cards

[Roost Cards](https://github.com/HRadhi4/Roost-card) are made to go with Athom Hub:

- **Roost Remote**: a full remote pad for an Athom Hub remote. Type the remote's name, done.
- **Roost Thermostat**: drag the AC's temperature with your finger.
- **Roost Curtain**: curtains that really slide, with a **Reverse** switch for motors that report the wrong way round.
- **Roost Presence Map**: the presence sensor's live room map.
